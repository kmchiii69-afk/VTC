## PART 3: THE WEBINAR SLIDE DECK / SCRIPT (→ WebinarJam + slides)

Mapped to the SOP's 20-step framework. Methodology: **The 3 S's — Structure, System, Signal.**

### Section A: Opening & Setup
1. **Opening & Welcome** — rapport, credentials, early testimonial. "If you're running a coaching or consulting business and your content isn't generating consistent sales, I'm going to show you how to add an additional $100K to your business, guaranteed. The system behind it pulls in $400K a month for us, organically. Not more posting. Not more hooks. A system. Before I do, quick proof: Wyatt added $305K in 90 days. Maya and Joey added $204K a month. Andrew added $139K a month."
2. **Set Clear Expectations** — ~[45–60] min; by the end you'll know the team structure, the feedback system, and the positioning shift; stay to the end for how to install it.
3. **Address Market Objections Immediately** — "I've tried hiring editors, it didn't work" / "I don't have time to manage a team" are symptoms of the same missing piece.
4. **Business Model Comparison** — SLIDE: "What Everyone Thinks Matters" (hooks, posting frequency, trending audio) vs "What Actually Matters" (team structure, feedback loop, positioning).
5. **Identify Pain Points & Desires** — poll: what's holding your content back? (no time / editing not matching level / posting but no clients / no system).

### Section B: Core Content — The 3 S's
6. **Introduce Core Methodology** — The 3 S's: Structure (team + accountability), System (how feedback moves), Signal (positioning so the market sees your worth).
7. **Step-by-Step Process** — 1) install structure, 2) install feedback system, 3) install positioning; in that order.
8. **Deep Dive: Structure** — the org chart: creative director → senior editor → junior editor. Clear chain of command, nobody's a bottleneck.
9. **Deep Dive: System (feedback loop)** — the fix isn't a better editor; it's a feedback system that resolves a revision in one round so quality doesn't live only in your head.
10. **Deep Dive: Signal (positioning)** — after 100+ coaches, the shared problem isn't posting volume; it's that content doesn't make them look worth what they charge. SLIDE: generic vs positioned content.
11. **Complete the Walkthrough** — Structure → unbottlenecked team; System → quality outside your head; Signal → content that converts. $500K in trial-and-error, given in 15 minutes.

### Section C: Social Proof & Pitch
12. **Success Stories** — Wyatt +$305K/90d, Maya & Joey +$204K/mo, Andrew +$139K/mo, Alessio & Bryan +$114K/mo, Josh +$101.5K/mo, Hans +$95K/mo, Andres +$72K/mo, Hoku +$65K/mo, Dario +$64K/30d. Nine businesses, same three installed.
13. **Challenge Objections** — time / money / experience.
14. **Transition to Offer (Bridge)** — build it yourself (months of trial and error) or apply for a free brand audit.
15. **Present Complete Solution** — the audit isn't a sales call in disguise; it's a teardown of where Structure/System/Signal is broken. [Deliverable: written breakdown / live teardown / recorded walkthrough]
16. **Benefits & Features** — maps team gaps → who to hire next; reviews last 10–20 pieces → where Signal breaks; hands you the SOP structure → quality out of your head.
17. **Urgency & Scarcity** — [only what's real: bonus for booking this week / spot cap / deadline]
18. **FAQs** — "I already have an editor" / "I don't have time to manage a team" / "How is this different from an agency" (+ 1–2 real ones from sales calls).
19. **Call to Action** — two options; apply for a free brand audit. Guarantee: add $100K running the $408K/mo system, or [WHAT HAPPENS IF THEY DON'T].
20. **Live Q&A** — answer simple questions live; funnel complex/off-topic to DM/email.
