## **1. My Entire Productivity System That Scaled My Business**

**Watch:** [https://www.instagram.com/reel/DYflADVsZQ2/](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DYflADVsZQ2/)

**Hook:** This is the exact weekly system I use to scale my business. It looks simple — but it completely changed my productivity.

### **Full script**

*This is my entire productivity system. Every single Monday, I take calls back to back to back. I try to stack as many as possible because I know that if I have one call in the middle of the day, I can't properly get into that deep workflow state. The key thing here is that you're stacking from back to front so that you maximize the amount of time you have in the morning and you're taking the calls as late as you can. On Tuesdays, I block out my calendar to not take calls at all. I'm writing scripts, I'm filming content, I'm hiring people, I'm creating systems — all of the things that actually move the needle forward. It's so important to block out these days because it can be so easy for calls to just stack up your calendar and it feels like your time is just disappearing. Now, what you'll notice is that I have three hours blocked out every single week in order to just learn, consume, and implement new information. As important as it is to work in the business and work on the business, it's as important to get new information from people that are ahead of you to implement so that you can actually grow faster. This is my entire system. It's simple, it's straightforward, and there's no fluff. That's how I've been able to scale to where I'm at today.*

### **Why it performed**

- The word “entire” promises a complete, copyable system — that completeness is what triggers saves, not just likes.
- “It looks simple but…” does two jobs at once: it lowers the barrier (this is achievable for me) while raising curiosity (so what's the catch?).
- Concrete tactical specifics — stack calls back-to-front, protect Tuesdays, three hours a week to learn — make it feel actionable and credible rather than motivational fluff.
- The closing signature “simple, straightforward, no fluff” is a recurring tonal stamp that positions him as the anti-guru. It builds trust by under-claiming.
- Feeds the ‘productive young operator’ pillar: proof he runs a real machine, not just posts about it.
