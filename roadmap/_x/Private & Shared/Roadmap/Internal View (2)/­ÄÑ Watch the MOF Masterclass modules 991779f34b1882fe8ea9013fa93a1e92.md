Action Items

1. Set up **a weekly 30-minute call** between your sales and content team, the only agenda item is: what objections came up this week?

2. Go through your last **10 onboarding forms** and find out WHY your clients chose to work with you. Turn these into your **next 3 pieces of content**

3. Write down the **5 most common objections** you hear on sales calls and identify the underlying belief behind each one

4. For each belief, outline **one piece of content** that shifts it through story or perspective - not argument

5. Write down the **3 most common limiting beliefs** your ICP holds and plan a piece of content for each that speaks to that belief from **your own experience**

6. Identify your **clearest point of difference** from everyone else in your space and make one piece of content this week that demonstrates it
